Challah Boards

For the Shabbat Table

An object that belongs at the centre of the table.

The challah board is one of the few Judaica objects that is both functional and always visible. It deserves to be something you want to look at — and something that will improve with time.

Our challah boards combine natural wood with amber inlay and smalt ornamentation. The compositions draw on traditional Jewish motifs — the pomegranate, the vine, wheat — rendered in a language that is contemporary rather than decorative.

Each board is made by hand. No two are identical. They make significant gifts for weddings, anniversaries, and dedications.
