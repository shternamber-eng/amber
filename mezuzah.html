Mezuzah Cases

For the Doorpost

The first object a home presents to the world.

The mezuzah is the most visible expression of a Jewish home. It deserves to be beautiful — not decorative-beautiful, but genuinely, materially beautiful. Made from something that has weight and warmth and depth.

Each of our mezuzah cases is hand-shaped from natural Baltic amber. Because no two pieces of amber are identical, no two cases are identical. The colour may run from pale honey to deep cognac. The form may be elongated or compact. What they share is the quality of the material and the attention given to each one.

Cases are available as individual commissions or as sets for multiple doorways. Custom engravings and metal fittings available on request.
