Torah Pointers

Yad — The Hand

Made to be held during the holiest reading.

The yad is an object of extraordinary intimacy — held by the hand, touching the most sacred text. It should feel like something. Not a piece of manufactured silver, but an object with presence and warmth.

Our Torah pointers are made from natural Baltic amber, selected for the warmth and depth of its colour. The amber handle is fitted to a silver or gold-filled tip, shaped as a hand with an extended finger — pointing, not touching. The combination of ancient organic material and precious metal is not decorative. It is right.

Each pointer is a unique piece. They are made as gifts for rabbis, scholars, and institutions — and as additions to synagogue ceremonial collections.
