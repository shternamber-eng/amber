Fundraising Art

For Galas & Campaigns

An artwork worthy of a significant gift.

When an institution asks its community for a major gift, the acknowledgement of that gift should reflect its significance. A framed certificate does not. An original artwork — made specifically for the occasion, from materials that carry their own history — does.

We create original amber and smalt artworks for institutional fundraising campaigns and gala events. Each piece is designed around the institution's theme, identity, and the occasion it marks. No edition prints. No reproductions. One piece, for one donor.

These works are also created for live auction at gala events — where the story of the materials and the process becomes part of the bidding experience.
