Donor Recognition Walls

Gratitude in Permanent Form

Recognition that honours rather than lists.

Donor recognition should feel like gratitude expressed with permanence — not a plaque on a wall, but an artwork that happens to carry names. The amber field creates a warmth and depth that no other material achieves. Names set within it feel held, not mounted.

Each installation is designed around the space it will inhabit. We work with the institution's leadership to understand the donors, the history, and the atmosphere the wall should create. The result is something that will still feel right in fifty years.
