The Studio

Objects of memory deserve more than function.

Shtern Amber was founded on a single conviction: that memorial and ceremonial art should carry genuine weight — in material, in craft, and in meaning.

We work without templates. Each commission begins with a conversation about the space, the community, and the story that the piece will hold. The result is something that cannot be replicated.

Our materials — natural Baltic amber from Ukraine, handmade smalt from Murano — are chosen because they are irreplaceable. Like the moments they commemorate.

Materials

Chosen for meaning,
not convenience.

◈

Natural Ukrainian Amber

Baltic amber, millions of years in formation, harvested from Ukrainian deposits. Warm, luminous, unrepeatable — each piece distinct in colour and inclusion.

◇

Italian Murano Smalt

Handmade glass from the island of Murano, produced by the same families that have made it for centuries. Used in mosaics since the Byzantine era.

○

Precious Metals & Stone

Select commissions incorporate gold leaf, sterling silver, and carved stone elements — chosen in dialogue with the client and the space.
