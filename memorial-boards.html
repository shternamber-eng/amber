Memorial Boards

Yahrzeit & Memorial

A place where names are held.

A memorial board is not signage. It is a place where a community stands and remembers. The names it holds deserve to be held with dignity — in materials that will endure, in a composition that honours rather than lists.

Each board is made entirely by hand. The amber panels are selected individually for colour and translucency. The individual name plaques are handcrafted from amber and fitted with illuminated Yahrzeit lights that can be activated on the day of remembrance.

We work with synagogues from the initial concept through to installation, adapting each design to the dimensions, character, and community of the specific space.
