The Studio

About Shtern Amber

The Founder

Uriel Shtern

Uriel Shtern was born in Odessa, Ukraine — a city that shaped his sense of beauty, craft, and Jewish identity in equal measure. Before founding the studio, he established NGO Kvitna, a charitable organization dedicated to women's health and early cancer detection, which he led for over a decade.

The studio grew from a private conviction: that the objects used in Jewish communal and home life deserve the same attention and permanence as the traditions they serve. Mass production cannot create that. Only hand and intention can.

Today, Uriel works with a small circle of craftspeople and suppliers — each chosen for their commitment to natural materials and irreplaceable technique.

What We Do

Every commission is a collaboration.

We create memorial boards and donor recognition installations for synagogues, schools, and community centres — and ceremonial objects for Jewish homes. Each piece begins with a conversation about the space, the community, and the people it will honour.

Institutional Work

Memorial boards, Yahrzeit plaques, donor recognition walls, and Tree of Life installations for synagogues and Jewish community spaces.

Home Objects

Mezuzah cases, Torah pointers, challah boards, and home blessing panels — objects made to be used, not displayed behind glass.
